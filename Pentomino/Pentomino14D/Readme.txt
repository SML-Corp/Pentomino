Pentomino 14mm Square Series

PenA14D12PCE.STL
This is a set of 12 pentomino pieces.
Each piece is shaped like five cubes with 14mm sides arranged together.

PenA14C610B.STL
Creates a 6x10 box. The body and lid are identical.
This is the prototype for the 2D Pentomino box-packing problem.
A French company has commercialized this as KATAMINO.

PenA14C562B.STL
Creates a 5x6x2 box. The body and lid are identical.
This becomes a 3D Pentomino. It is about 50 times more difficult than the 6x10 version.

PenA14C453C.STL
Create a 4x5x3 box with identical body and lid.
This becomes a 3D Pentomino, but it is about 50 times more difficult than the 5x6x2 version.

Good luck.
